--- title: "Hire Your Most Vocal Customers" subtitle: "The people who complain the loudest often care the most. Some of them make the best teammates." description: "The people who complain the loudest often care the most. Some of them make the best teammates." read_time: "4 min read" ---

Every business has a few customers who write long emails, show up in the forums, and tell you exactly what is wrong. It is tempting to see them as a cost.

In my experience they are a hiring pool. At Event Espresso we ended up hiring some of our more vocal and involved customers: people who used our plugin, or used it to build sites for their own clients. Several of our support engineers joined the team after hanging out in our forums and answering other customers' questions. Two of our core developers started by asking questions in the support forum, and ended up answering one of our job postings.

It makes sense when you think about it. These people already know the product. They have already shown they care. And they have already done a version of the job for free.

It does not remove the need to hire carefully. Hiring the wrong people is one of the hardest lessons a business learns, so we look for people who fit the company's culture, values and long-term vision, and we have habits that keep everyone honest, like having a second person review code and support conversations.

But if you are wondering where your next good hire might come from, look at who is already talking to you. The loudest voice in your inbox might be your next teammate.

About

Twenty-five years, still building.

I have been building websites for over 25 years and running businesses even longer. I like making tools that help a business thrive, or give people a way to be creative.

After that I bought a garage door company, grew its search traffic and online presence, then sold it and went back to WordPress products.

Today I'm a Senior Project Manager for several leading WordPress tools, including AccessAlly, MemberMouse, Wishlist Member and PrettyLinks. I also run the monthly AI and WordPress meetup, and I build with a team of AI agents I created.

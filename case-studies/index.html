Local service company

A+ Garage Doors: three years of AI, working in the background.

The problem. A garage door company needs to show up in every neighborhood it serves, and its staff need answers fast.

What we built. Research-backed pages for each neighborhood, and a report for every one showing where it ranks in Google, so we can see what's working. Apps for the warehouse and the sales team. A company knowledge base the team asks questions of, instead of hunting through documents.

How it works day to day. I've worked alongside the company for three years, so the tools grow with the business instead of being handed over once.

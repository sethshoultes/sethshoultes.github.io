Local service company

A+ Garage Doors: three years of AI, working in the background.

The problem. A garage door company needs to show up in every neighborhood it serves, and its staff need answers fast.

What we built. Research-backed pages for each neighborhood, and a report for every one showing where it ranks in Google, so we can see what's working. Apps for the warehouse and the sales team. A company knowledge base the team asks questions of, instead of hunting through documents.

How it works day to day. I've worked alongside the company for three years, so the tools grow with the business instead of being handed over once.

Agent pipeline

Shipyard AI: five weeks of PRD in, production out.

The idea. Write down what you want as a product requirements document, and a team of AI agents debates it, plans it, builds it, tests it, reviews it and ships it. Each step is run by a named agent, and the pipeline checks the build before anything ships.

What we built. An autonomous pipeline of twelve steps, from a PRD check at the front to the ship at the end, that makes EmDash sites and plugins. A clean run takes about 35 to 45 minutes.

The numbers. The ship log lists 85 shipped PRDs, from April 6 to May 12, 2026. It also keeps a record of 12 failed ones, with the cause of each. In the EmDash plugin suite, EventDash works today and the others are in progress.

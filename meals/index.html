The Shoultes family meal planner

Pick the week's dinners and the grocery list builds itself, grouped by store. These are the meals we actually cook. It saves in this browser, so there is nothing to sign in to.

This week

Choose a meal for each day, type your own, or drag a meal from further down onto a day. Add a note for the things that are not dinner, like soccer or guild.

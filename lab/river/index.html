About this prototype

How it works

What it is
A ten-second flight down a winding desert river. Where you are on the page decides which moment you see, and each bend holds one project.
How heavy
The page itself is under 60 KB. The video comes after the page is ready: 504 KB in the small format, 590 KB for browsers that need the older one.
How fast
100 out of 100 on Google's mobile speed test, measured on my laptop.
If you want less motion
Phones and laptops can ask for it. Then the video never loads: you get one still picture and plain scrolling.
The footage
Made with AI: a general view of desert river country, not a photo and not a named place. I'll swap in real footage of the Virgin River.

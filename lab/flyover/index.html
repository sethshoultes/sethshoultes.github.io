About this prototype

How it works

What it is
A ten-second flight over high forest and red rock country. Where you are on the page decides which moment you see, so scrolling down flies you forward and scrolling up flies you back. Each stop holds a group of posts.
How heavy
The page itself is under 60 KB. The video comes after the page is ready: 786 KB in the small format, 821 KB for browsers that need the older one.
How fast
100 out of 100 on Google's mobile speed test, measured on my laptop.
If you want less motion
Phones and laptops can ask for it. Then the video never loads: you get one still picture and plain scrolling.
The footage
Made with AI: a general view of southern Utah country, not a photo and not a named place. I'll swap in drone footage of the real route when I have it.

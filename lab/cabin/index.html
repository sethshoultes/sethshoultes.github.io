About this prototype

How it works

What it is
A ten-second time-lapse from a cabin porch, from dawn to dusk. Where you are on the page decides which moment you see, so the light changes as you read.
How heavy
The page itself is under 60 KB. The video comes after the page is ready: 621 KB in the small format, 494 KB for browsers that need the older one.
How fast
100 out of 100 on Google's mobile speed test, measured on my laptop.
If you want less motion
Phones and laptops can ask for it. Then the video never loads: you get one still picture and plain scrolling.
The footage
Made with AI: a stand-in for the view from my cabin near Kolob Reservoir, not a photo of it. I'll swap in my own footage.

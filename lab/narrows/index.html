About this prototype

How it works

Technique
One ten-second clip of a canyon river. Your scroll position sets which frame shows, so scrolling down walks you forward and scrolling up walks you back.
Weight
Page and poster: 317 KB. The clip loads after the page is usable: 684 KB as WebM (AV1), 801 KB as MP4 for browsers that need it.
Lighthouse mobile
99 performance (measured locally, simulated mobile).
Reduced motion
If your device asks for less motion, the clip never loads. You get the still poster and plain scrolling.
Footage
Generated, not a photo of a real place. It will be swapped for Seth's own video if he shares it.

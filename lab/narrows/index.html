About this prototype

How it works

What it is
One ten-second video of a canyon river. Where you are on the page decides which moment you see. Scroll down and you walk forward. Scroll up and you walk back.
How heavy
The page itself is under 60 KB. The video comes after the page is ready: 684 KB in the small format, 801 KB for browsers that need the older one.
How fast
99 out of 100 on Google's mobile speed test, measured on my laptop.
If you want less motion
Phones and laptops can ask for it. Then the video never loads: you get one still picture and plain scrolling.
The footage
Made with AI, not a photo of a real place. I'll swap in my own video from the canyon.

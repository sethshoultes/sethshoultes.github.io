8. Aerial Photography and Videography

{% raw %}

Drones have revolutionized photography and videography, allowing you to capture breathtaking aerial shots that were previously impossible. Here are some techniques to master:

  • Framing and Composition: Learn to use the rule of thirds and leading lines when framing shots from the sky.
  • Camera Settings: Manually adjust ISO, shutter speed, and aperture to control exposure and capture sharper images, especially in low-light conditions.
  • Smooth Movements: Jerky movements can ruin footage. Practice smooth, slow pans and tilts to make your videos look professional.
  • Advanced Techniques:
    • Parallax Shots: Achieved by flying the drone sideways while keeping the camera focused on a moving subject, creating a dynamic effect.
    • Dolly Zoom: A camera effect where you zoom in while the drone moves away, creating a unique distortion known as the "vertigo effect."

Pro Tip:

Use autonomous flight modes like Waypoint Navigation to capture consistent shots over a specific route. For instance, set waypoints around a landmark to film it from multiple angles.

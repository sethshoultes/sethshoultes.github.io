Drone Piloting: Basic Concepts

Battery Management and Signal Loss

{% raw %}

As you venture further into piloting, you’ll need to be more conscious of your drone’s battery and connection.

  • Battery Life: Intermediate pilots should monitor battery life carefully. Drones often return to home automatically when the battery is low, but understanding power consumption based on speed, maneuvers, and wind resistance is key.
  • Signal Loss: If your drone loses connection, most modern drones will either hover in place or return to home. However, if the auto-return fails, you must be prepared to regain manual control.

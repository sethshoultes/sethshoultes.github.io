Academy / Drone Piloting: Getting Started

Advanced Maneuvers and Techniques

Immelmann Turn

{% raw %}

This maneuver combines a half-loop with a half-roll, allowing the drone to quickly change direction at higher altitudes. The Immelmann Turn is perfect for situations where you need to reverse course while gaining altitude.

How to Perform an Immelmann Turn:

  1. Start flying level, then pitch back to go into a vertical climb.
  2. Once you're vertical, perform a half-roll to level the drone.
  3. You’ll now be flying in the opposite direction at a higher altitude.

Practical Use:

This move is particularly useful in drone racing or obstacle courses, where quick, precise changes in direction are needed without losing altitude.

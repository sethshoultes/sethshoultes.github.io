Drone Piloting: Advanced Concepts

Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) Operations

{% raw %}

Flying Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) is one of the most advanced forms of drone operations and requires special certifications in many countries due to its complexity and increased risk.

  • Uses: BVLOS flights are often used in delivery services, pipeline inspections, or agriculture, where the drone covers long distances without being in the pilot’s direct line of sight.
  • Challenges:
    • Communication Range: You’ll need stronger communication systems to maintain control over long distances.
    • Legal Restrictions: Many regions require special permits and safety protocols for BVLOS flights to ensure the drone does not interfere with manned aircraft.

Example:

Amazon is actively testing drone deliveries, leveraging BVLOS to send packages autonomously over long distances to customers.

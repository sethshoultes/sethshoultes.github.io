Drone Piloting: Advanced Concepts

Drone Laws and Certifications

{% raw %}

Once you reach the advanced stage, you’ll likely be flying drones for professional purposes, which brings new legal considerations.

  • Licensing: In many countries, commercial drone pilots are required to have certifications, such as the Part 107 in the United States, which involves passing a knowledge test.
  • Airspace Regulations: Learn about controlled and uncontrolled airspace, Temporary Flight Restrictions (TFRs), and how to read aeronautical charts.

Example:

If you’re planning to shoot drone footage for a film project near an airport, you’ll need to understand the airspace regulations and potentially request permission from the local aviation authority.

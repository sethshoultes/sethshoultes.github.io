Drone Piloting: Basic Concepts

Emergency Procedures

{% raw %}

Things can go wrong, so knowing how to handle emergencies is crucial:

  • Loss of Signal: Stay calm, check the drone’s position, and attempt to re-establish a connection by moving closer.
  • Low Battery Return: If the battery gets critically low, land the drone immediately, avoiding long-distance returns.
  • Compass/GPS Failure: Switch to manual mode, maintain a safe altitude, and gently land the drone.

Tip:

Always keep an eye on the drone’s distance and battery status through your controller's display, so you can respond quickly to emergencies.

Drone Piloting: Basic Concepts

Flying in Different Conditions

{% raw %}

A drone pilot must handle more complex environments and conditions:

  • Wind: Even mild wind can impact flight. Learning to compensate for drift is crucial.
  • Obstacles: When flying around trees, buildings, or other structures, be aware of potential turbulence and GPS interference.
  • Indoor Flying: Without GPS stabilization, flying indoors requires more manual control and quick reaction times.

Pro Tip:

When flying in windy conditions, use GPS mode (if available) to stabilize your drone, but always be ready to switch to manual control in case of a GPS error.

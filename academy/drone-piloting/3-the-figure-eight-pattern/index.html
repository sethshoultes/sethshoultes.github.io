Academy / Drone Piloting: Getting Started

Advanced Maneuvers and Techniques

The Figure-Eight Pattern

{% raw %}

This maneuver helps develop precise control over your drone’s orientation and path. The figure-eight pattern challenges you to combine yaw, pitch, and roll while changing directions, making it a perfect training exercise for building smooth flying skills.

How to Fly a Figure-Eight:

  1. Start by flying the drone forward in a wide circle.
  2. As you reach the halfway point, smoothly transition into a yaw and roll in the opposite direction, making the second half of the figure.
  3. Combine the movements so the drone completes the loop smoothly, with no jerky motions.

Training Tip:

This drill improves your spatial awareness, especially when the drone is facing you, which can be disorienting as your controls are reversed.

Drone Piloting: Basic Concepts

Controls and Flight Techniques

{% raw %}

In drone piloting, you're no longer just making the drone go up and down. You'll learn to combine multiple control inputs simultaneously to achieve smooth, controlled flights.

  • Left Stick: Usually controls throttle (up and down) and yaw (rotate left/right).
  • Right Stick: Controls pitch (forward/backward) and roll (side to side).

Practice Tip:

Try making figure-eight patterns, combining yaw, pitch, and roll for smoother transitions. This teaches you how to handle the drone’s direction and orientation when it’s flying towards you, which can be tricky!

Advanced Maneuvers and Techniques

Tight Turns (Slalom Flying)

{% raw %}

Slalom flying involves flying in and out of obstacles in quick succession. This is crucial for drone racing or navigating tight spaces in real-world applications like inspections or search-and-rescue operations.

How to Perform Tight Turns:

  1. Start by flying straight toward a marker (like a tree or pylon).
  2. As you approach the marker, yaw in the desired direction and apply slight roll to shift the drone to the side.
  3. Quickly alternate between yawing left and right as you pass through a series of obstacles.

Practice Tip:

Set up small obstacles like cones or poles and practice weaving between them to sharpen your reflexes and maneuvering skills.

5. The "Split-S" Maneuver

{% raw %}

The Split-S is a quick, efficient way to reverse direction while maintaining speed. This maneuver is commonly used in drone racing but can also be applied in other flight scenarios.

How to Perform a Split-S:

  1. Begin flying forward at speed.
  2. Roll the drone 180 degrees so it’s upside down.
  3. Pull back on the right stick (pitch) to complete a half-loop and face the opposite direction.

Example:

If you're flying towards an obstacle, instead of stopping and turning around, a Split-S allows you to quickly reverse direction and continue flying.

Academy / MemberPress & Event Espresso for Non-profit Organizations

Examples: How Non-Profits Can Grow Through Technology Integrations

8. Website Chatbots for Donor and Volunteer Support

{% raw %}

Tools: Intercom, Drift, Zapier

How It Works:

  • Intercom or Drift can be used to set up chatbots on your non-profit’s website to assist visitors with frequently asked questions, event registrations, or donation inquiries.
  • Integrating these chat tools with Zapier allows the chatbot to automatically record conversations, add leads to your email list, and follow up with donors or volunteers.
  • For example, if someone asks how they can donate, the chatbot can guide them to a donation page and trigger a follow-up email with more information about your non-profit’s impact.

Example Growth Impact:

  • Improved Donor Experience: Visitors can get their questions answered immediately, without having to wait for staff. This can increase conversions from interested visitors to donors.
  • Volunteer Support: Volunteers can use the chatbot to sign up for events or get more information about available shifts, improving their engagement with your organization.

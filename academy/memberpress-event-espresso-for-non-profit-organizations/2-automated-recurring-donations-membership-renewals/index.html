2. Automated Recurring Donations & Membership Renewals

{% raw %}

Tools: MemberPress, Stripe, Zapier

How It Works:

  • Use MemberPress to manage memberships and recurring donations on your non-profit’s website.
  • By integrating Stripe (or another payment gateway) with MemberPress, you can automate recurring donations. This ensures that donors don’t need to manually renew their contributions every month or year.
  • Zapier can be used to trigger personalized thank-you emails, reminders, or even exclusive member content based on their recurring donation activity.

Example Growth Impact:

  • Increased Retention: Automating renewals ensures that members don’t forget to donate again, increasing retention rates.
  • Seamless User Experience: Donors can manage their subscriptions, update payment details, or change membership tiers easily through an integrated platform, reducing churn.

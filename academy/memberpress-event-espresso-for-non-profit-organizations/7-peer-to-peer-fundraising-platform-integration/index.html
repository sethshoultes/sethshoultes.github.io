Examples: How Non-Profits Can Grow Through Technology Integrations

7. Peer-to-Peer Fundraising Platform Integration

{% raw %}

Tools: Classy, GiveWP, Zapier

How It Works:

  • Classy or GiveWP are peer-to-peer fundraising platforms that allow your supporters to create their own fundraising campaigns on your behalf.
  • Integrating these platforms with Zapier allows you to automatically update your CRM or email platform when a new peer fundraiser is created, or when donations come in from those campaigns.
  • You can trigger automated thank-you emails, notifications to the peer fundraiser, and real-time updates on their progress.

Example Growth Impact:

  • Expands Reach: Peer-to-peer fundraising allows your most engaged supporters to raise money on your behalf, expanding your reach to their networks.
  • Automated Engagement: Every time a donation is made to a peer fundraising campaign, the donor and the fundraiser can receive personalized thank-you messages, encouraging them to stay engaged.

Academy / MemberPress & Event Espresso for Non-profit Organizations

Real-World Simulation: Managing Memberships and Events for a Non-Profit

Step 3: Integration Linking MemberPress and Event Espresso

{% raw %}

Scenario: You want to ensure that your members automatically receive discounts or free access to the gala based on their membership level.

Action Plan:

  1. Set Up Membership Discounts:
    • In Event Espresso, create discount codes or free access for specific membership levels.
    • Use the MemberPress integration to verify member status at the time of ticket purchase.
    • Automatically apply a discount code for Wolf Protectors or grant free tickets for Bear Guardians.
  2. Track Attendance and Membership:
    • Use Event Espresso’s reporting tools to see how many of your members have registered for the event.
    • If some members have not signed up, use the automated email reminder system in both MemberPress and Event Espresso to nudge them.

Result:

The integration ensures that members enjoy their perks seamlessly. Your gala is filling up with attendees, and you’ve strengthened your relationship with key supporters by recognizing their contribution with special access or discounts.

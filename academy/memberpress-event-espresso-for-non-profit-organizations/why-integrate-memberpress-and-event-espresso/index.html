Deeper Look at Integration: MemberPress and Event Espresso for Non-Profits

Why Integrate MemberPress and Event Espresso?

{% raw %}

Let’s explore a deeper integration between MemberPress and Event Espresso, which can help your non-profit run smoother by automating processes and providing a seamless experience for both members and event attendees.

Integrating these two tools allows your non-profit to:

  • Offer event perks based on membership levels (e.g., discounted or free event access).
  • Automatically verify memberships when attendees register for events.
  • Create a unified experience for members, ensuring they feel recognized and valued.

With the integration in place, you don’t have to manually manage member privileges for events or cross-reference lists. Everything becomes automated and tied together, saving your team time and reducing the risk of human error.

Examples: How Non-Profits Can Grow Through Technology Integrations

Volunteer Management & Engagement Automation

{% raw %}

Tools: Volgistics, Mailchimp, Slack, Zapier

How It Works:

  • Volgistics is a volunteer management system that helps non-profits organize volunteer shifts, track hours, and communicate with volunteers.
  • By integrating Volgistics with Mailchimp, you can automate volunteer onboarding and engagement emails (e.g., sending training materials to new volunteers).
  • Integrating Volgistics with Slack ensures that team leaders are notified immediately when shifts are filled or when volunteers complete their tasks.

Example Growth Impact:

  • Increased Volunteer Retention: Automating the communication process (reminders, appreciation emails, training) keeps volunteers engaged, leading to higher retention rates.
  • Improved Coordination: Automated notifications in Slack help team leaders stay organized, allowing for more efficient use of volunteers’ time.

Fundamental Concepts: Membership and Event Management for Non-Profits

3. Combining Membership and Events

{% raw %}

By linking membership and event management, non-profits can amplify their impact:

  • Incentivizing Memberships: Offering members discounts or exclusive access to events encourages more supporters to join as members.
  • Building Community: Events can act as meeting points for members, fostering a sense of community and shared purpose.
  • Recurring Engagement: Events provide ongoing touchpoints for non-profits to engage with their membership base, ensuring continued involvement and financial support.

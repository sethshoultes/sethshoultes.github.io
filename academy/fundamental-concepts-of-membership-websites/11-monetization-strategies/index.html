Guide to Building an Online Learning Platform Using MemberPress

11. Monetization Strategies

{% raw %}

MemberPress allows you to monetize your learning platform in various ways:

Subscription Plans:

  • Offer monthly or annual subscriptions for access to all courses.
  • Create different tiers (Basic, Premium) with varying levels of access.

One-Time Course Purchases:

  • Sell individual courses with a one-time payment option.
  • Combine this with lifetime access to certain content or a bundled package of courses.

Freemium Model:

  • Offer a few lessons or introductory courses for free, with premium courses available for paying members.

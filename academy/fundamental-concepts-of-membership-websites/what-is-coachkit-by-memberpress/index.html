Expanding Your Coaching Business with CoachKit by MemberPress

What is CoachKit by MemberPress?

{% raw %}

CoachKit by MemberPress is a powerful tool for coaches looking to grow and manage their online coaching business. It simplifies managing clients, delivering content, offering memberships, and even automating many aspects of client interaction and business administration. Whether you're a life coach, fitness trainer, business mentor, or health coach, CoachKit equips you with all the necessary features to create, deliver, and monetize your coaching programs.

Here’s a comprehensive guide to using CoachKit by MemberPress to build a thriving coaching business.

What is CoachKit by MemberPress?

CoachKit is an extension of MemberPress specifically designed for coaches, providing all the tools needed to manage client sessions, sell coaching packages, deliver courses, offer membership subscriptions, and track client progress.

Key Features of CoachKit:

  • Client Management: Simplify bookings, scheduling, and client interaction.
  • Coaching Packages: Offer various types of coaching (e.g., one-on-one, group, session packages).
  • Courses and Memberships: Create membership tiers and drip content through courses and resources.
  • Progress Tracking: Keep track of client activities, course completions, and overall progress.
  • Monetization: Sell coaching services, online courses, and digital products directly through your platform.

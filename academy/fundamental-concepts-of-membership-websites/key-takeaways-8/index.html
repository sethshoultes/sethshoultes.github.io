Adding Video Chats and AI to Your Dating Website

Key Takeaways

{% raw %}
  1. Video Chats: Integrate video chats using plugins like CometChat or services like Twilio to provide real-time interactions, enhancing user trust and engagement.
  2. AI Matchmaking: Leverage AI plugins like MatchMaker AI or Findmate AI to create smarter, personalized matchmaking based on user behavior and preferences.
  3. Monetization: Charge users for access to premium video chat features or offer AI-driven match insights and search tools to paying members.

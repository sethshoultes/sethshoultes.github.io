Building a Hiking Membership Website Using MemberPress

Create and Organize Hiking Content

{% raw %}

Your content is what will attract and retain members. You can create different types of content like trail guides, hiking tips, gear reviews, or training plans, and categorize it based on your membership levels.

Step 5.1 Add Trail Guides

  1. Go to Posts → Add New and create detailed hiking trail guides.
  2. Include important information:
    • Trail Difficulty: Easy, moderate, or difficult.
    • Distance and Elevation: Include metrics like distance, elevation gain, and estimated hiking time.
    • GPS Coordinates: Provide GPS locations and map routes.
    • Photos: Add high-quality images of the trail.

Step 5.2 Offer Hiking Tips and Tutorials

  • Create blog posts or pages dedicated to hiking tips, gear reviews, or training plans.
  • For premium members, consider offering video tutorials on advanced hiking techniques or safety training.

Expanding Your Coaching Business with CoachKit by MemberPress

7. Email Marketing and Client Engagement

{% raw %}

Keep your clients engaged and informed by integrating email marketing into your coaching platform.

7.1 Automated Emails and Drip Campaigns

Send regular newsletters, course updates, and coaching tips through an email marketing service like MailChimp or ConvertKit.

  1. Set up automated drip email campaigns that send resources, tips, or encouragement at certain intervals during your client’s coaching journey.
  2. Use these emails to promote new coaching packages, courses, or special events.

7.2 Personalized Client Communication

You can create email reminders for key coaching sessions, follow-ups after calls, or customized check-ins using MemberPress’s built-in email reminders.

Pro Tip: Add a personal touch to your emails by addressing specific challenges or goals that your client has mentioned in previous coaching sessions.

Monetizing AI Features

{% raw %}

Here’s how you can monetize AI-powered features on your dating website:

  • Premium Match Insights: Offer users detailed breakdowns of why they matched with someone, available to premium members only.
  • AI-Powered Search Filters: Allow paid users to access advanced search filters and AI-generated match suggestions.
  • Customizable Matches: Let premium users customize AI algorithms based on their exact preferences (e.g., prioritize distance or interests).

Academy / Building Membership Websites with WordPress

Guide to Building an Online Learning Platform Using MemberPress

Email Notifications and Drip Content

{% raw %}

Email notifications and drip content are essential to keep learners engaged and ensure they follow through with your course material.

Set Up Email Reminders

  • Go to MemberPress → Emails to configure automated email reminders.
  • Send students notifications about course progress, incomplete lessons, or upcoming quizzes.
  • You can also notify members of expiring memberships or course discounts.

Drip Content for Lessons

  • Drip content allows you to release course material over time, preventing users from accessing the entire course at once.
  • Go to MemberPress → Rules → Drip.
  • Set the timing for when lessons or quizzes should be released (e.g., release a new lesson every week).

Why Use Drip Content?: Drip-feeding lessons can help keep students engaged for longer periods and encourage regular visits to your platform.

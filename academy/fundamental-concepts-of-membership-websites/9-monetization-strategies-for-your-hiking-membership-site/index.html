Building a Hiking Membership Website Using MemberPress

Monetization Strategies for Your Hiking Membership Site

{% raw %}

In addition to selling memberships, you can add other revenue streams to grow your business.

9.1 Sell Digital Products

Offer downloadable hiking maps, trail guides, or e-books about hiking, which can be sold as one-time purchases to both members and non-members.

9.2 Affiliate Marketing

Promote hiking gear or outdoor equipment from brands you trust. Use affiliate links in your gear review blog posts to earn a commission when members buy products through your links.

9.3 Sell Merchandise

Integrate WooCommerce to sell hiking-related merchandise like branded water bottles, backpacks, or apparel directly from your website.

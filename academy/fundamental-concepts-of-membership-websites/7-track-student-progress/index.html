Academy / Building Membership Websites with WordPress

Guide to Building an Online Learning Platform Using MemberPress

Track Student Progress

{% raw %}

MemberPress Courses provides built-in progress tracking, so you and your students can monitor progress throughout the course.

Student Dashboard

  • Students can see their course progress in a dedicated dashboard that shows completed lessons, quiz scores, and certificates earned.
  • They can easily pick up where they left off in any course.

Admin Reports

  • As the admin, you can track user progress by going to MemberPress → Reports.
  • View how many students have completed certain courses, quiz scores, and which membership levels are the most popular.
{% endraw %}

Uncanny Automator with MemberPress

Examples of Common Automations:

{% raw %}
  1. Send a Welcome Email:
    • When a new user signs up for a membership in MemberPress, Uncanny Automator can automatically send a personalized welcome email through your email marketing platform.
  2. Grant Access to a Course:
    • When a user purchases a certain membership, automatically enroll them in a specific online course or group in your LMS.
  3. Notify Admins of Cancellations:
    • If a user cancels their subscription in MemberPress, you can trigger an automatic notification to your site admins for follow-up.

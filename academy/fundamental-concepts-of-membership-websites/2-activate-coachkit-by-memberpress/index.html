Academy / Building Membership Websites with WordPress

Expanding Your Coaching Business with CoachKit by MemberPress

2. Activate CoachKit by MemberPress

{% raw %}

Once you have MemberPress installed, it’s time to enable CoachKit to access its coaching-specific tools.

  1. Go to WordPress Dashboard → MemberPress → Add-Ons.
  2. Locate the CoachKit add-on and click Install Add-On.
  3. Once installed, activate CoachKit from your dashboard.

This unlocks all the CoachKit features within MemberPress, allowing you to manage clients, create coaching packages, and offer subscription services.

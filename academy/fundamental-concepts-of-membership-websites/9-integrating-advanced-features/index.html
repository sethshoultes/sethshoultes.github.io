Academy / Building Membership Websites with WordPress

Expanding Your Coaching Business with CoachKit by MemberPress

9. Integrating Advanced Features

{% raw %}

Once your business starts growing, you can scale further by adding advanced features to your coaching platform:

9.1 Membership Upgrades

Allow clients to upgrade memberships easily. For instance, a client might start with a basic plan but may want to upgrade to premium after experiencing your services.

9.2 Multi-Instructor Setup

As your business expands, consider allowing other coaches to offer their services on your platform. You can create instructor profiles and sell their coaching services under your brand.

9.3 Create a Mobile App

For an even more convenient user experience, develop a mobile app where clients can book sessions, access courses, and track their progress on the go.

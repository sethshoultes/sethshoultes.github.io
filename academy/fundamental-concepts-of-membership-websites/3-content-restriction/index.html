Fundamental Concepts of Membership Websites

3. Content Restriction

{% raw %}

Restricting content means limiting access to certain parts of your website based on membership levels. This allows you to create valuable gated content that drives users to upgrade their membership.

  • Protected Pages/Posts: Set rules in your membership plugin (like MemberPress) to restrict specific content for higher-tier members.
  • Courses & Products: Offer premium online courses, digital downloads, or members-only webinars.
  • Communities/Forums: Create exclusive discussion groups or private forums for paying members.

Example: On a fitness website, free users can access blog posts, but only premium members get workout plans, videos, or direct consultations.

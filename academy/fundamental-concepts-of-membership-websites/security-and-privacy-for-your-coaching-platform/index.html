Security and Privacy for Your Coaching Platform

{% raw %}

Ensure that your clients’ data and personal information are protected:

  • Install an SSL Certificate for encrypted data transfers.
  • Use Wordfence or iThemes Security to protect your WordPress site from hacking.
  • Ensure your platform is GDPR-compliant, particularly if you serve clients in the EU.

Expanding Your Coaching Business with CoachKit by MemberPress

5. Create and Sell Online Courses with CoachKit

{% raw %}

One powerful way to expand your coaching business is by offering online courses that complement your coaching sessions. You can sell these courses as standalone products or bundle them with coaching packages.

5.1 Create a Course with MemberPress Courses

CoachKit integrates seamlessly with MemberPress Courses, making it easy to build courses with lessons and quizzes.

  1. Go to MemberPress → Courses → Add New.
  2. Create a new course related to your coaching niche (e.g., “Mindset Mastery” or “Business Growth Fundamentals”).
  3. Add lessons, videos, worksheets, and quizzes.
  4. Drip content over time, so clients work through the course at a pace that aligns with your coaching plan.

5.2 Bundle Courses with Coaching Packages

You can sell courses as part of your coaching packages, giving clients access to educational content between sessions.

  1. Go to MemberPress → Rules.
  2. Create a rule to restrict access to certain courses based on membership levels.
  3. For example, only VIP coaching members could get access to advanced courses, while basic members get entry-level courses.

Pro Tip: Offer courses as a self-paced learning option for clients who want to start at their own convenience but still offer personalized feedback through your coaching.

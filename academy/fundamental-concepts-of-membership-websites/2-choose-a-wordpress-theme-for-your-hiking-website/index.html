Building a Hiking Membership Website Using MemberPress

Choose a WordPress Theme for Your Hiking Website

{% raw %}

A good theme can set the tone and functionality of your site. Choose a theme that is visually appealing, easy to navigate, and responsive across devices (mobile, tablet, and desktop).

Recommended Themes:

  • Astra: Lightweight, customizable, and compatible with page builders.
  • OceanWP: Great for outdoor or nature-based websites.
  • GeneratePress: Perfect for building fast and flexible websites.

Once you've chosen a theme:

  • Install: Go to Appearance → Themes → Add New and search for your chosen theme.
  • Activate: Once installed, activate your theme and begin customizing it.

Expanding Your Coaching Business with CoachKit by MemberPress

Track Client Progress

{% raw %}

CoachKit makes it easy to keep track of your clients’ progress—whether they’re completing courses, attending sessions, or reaching specific goals.

6.1 Progress Tracking for Courses

With MemberPress Courses, both you and your clients can track course progress.

  1. Clients can see how many lessons they’ve completed and what’s left.
  2. You can monitor quiz results and engagement levels, helping you personalize future coaching sessions based on their progress.

6.2 Set Client Milestones

Set specific goals or milestones for your clients to achieve, which can be tracked over time.

  • Use tools like GamiPress to gamify the coaching experience by awarding badges or points for completed tasks, quizzes, or milestones.
  • Reward clients who complete courses or hit coaching goals with certificates, prizes, or exclusive content.

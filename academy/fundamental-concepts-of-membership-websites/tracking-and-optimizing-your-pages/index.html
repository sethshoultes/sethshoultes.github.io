Once your customizations are live, use analytics tools to monitor user engagement and optimize your pages for better conversion rates.

  • Google Analytics: Set up conversion tracking to monitor how many visitors convert into paying members.
  • A/B Testing: Test different variations of your pricing page (e.g., CTA text, layout, or colors) to see which design performs better.

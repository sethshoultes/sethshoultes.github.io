Fundamental Concepts of Membership Websites

Automation and Integrations

{% raw %}

Automating workflows can simplify running your membership website.

  • Payment Automation: Set up recurring billing to ensure seamless payments without manual intervention.
  • Email Automations: Use integrations like MailChimp or ActiveCampaign to send automated welcome emails, subscription renewal reminders, or abandoned cart emails.
  • CRM Integration: Use Customer Relationship Management (CRM) tools like HubSpot to manage member information and improve customer support.

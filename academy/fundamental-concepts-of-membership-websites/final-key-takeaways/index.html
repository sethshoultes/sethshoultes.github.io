Academy / Building Membership Websites with WordPress

Guide to Building an Online Learning Platform Using MemberPress

Final Key Takeaways:

{% raw %}
  1. MemberPress + Courses makes it easy to create an all-in-one online learning platform without additional LMS plugins.
  2. Customize courses, lessons, and quizzes with the built-in drag-and-drop editor.
  3. Restrict content access based on membership levels to monetize courses effectively.
  4. Automate drip content and email reminders to keep students engaged.
  5. Award certificates to students upon course completion to increase motivation.
  6. Track progress and analyze performance using built-in reporting features.
{% endraw %}

Building a Hiking Membership Website Using MemberPress

Email Marketing and Engagement

{% raw %}

Use email marketing to keep members engaged, inform them about new trails, upcoming events, and exclusive offers.

Step 7.1 Integrate an Email Marketing Tool

  • MailChimp or ConvertKit can help you automate emails and newsletters.
  • Set up automated welcome emails for new members and notify them about upcoming events, newly added trail guides, and important updates.

Step 7.2 Send Regular Updates

  • Drip Content: Release exclusive content (e.g., a new trail guide or tip) on a schedule for paid members.
  • Reminders: Send reminders for upcoming hikes or gear workshops to keep your members engaged.

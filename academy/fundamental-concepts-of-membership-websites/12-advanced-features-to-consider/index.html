Guide to Building an Online Learning Platform Using MemberPress

12. Advanced Features to Consider

{% raw %}

Once your platform is up and running, you can explore these advanced features:

  • Affiliate Program: Use the Affiliate Royale add-on to create an affiliate marketing program, allowing others to promote your courses and earn commissions.
  • Mobile App: Consider developing a mobile app for students who prefer learning on mobile devices.
  • Multi-Instructor Platform: Expand by allowing other instructors to upload and manage their own courses on your platform.

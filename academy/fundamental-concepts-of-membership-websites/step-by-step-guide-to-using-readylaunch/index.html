Customizing Your MemberPress Website Using ReadyLaunch

Step-by-Step Guide to Using ReadyLaunch

{% raw %}

1. 🔄 Activate ReadyLaunch in MemberPress

Before you start customizing, you need to make sure that ReadyLaunch is activated.

  1. Go to the WordPress Dashboard.
  2. Navigate to MemberPress → Settings.
  3. Click on the ReadyLaunch™ tab.
  4. Activate ReadyLaunch by switching the toggle on.

This activation will automatically apply the ReadyLaunch templates to your core MemberPress pages like sign-up, login, and account pages.

2. 🎨 Customize Your MemberPress Pages Using ReadyLaunch

Once ReadyLaunch is active, you can start customizing the pre-built templates to fit your brand and design preferences.

2.1 Customize Sign-Up Pages

  • Go to MemberPress → Memberships.
  • Click Edit on a specific membership level.
  • Under the Membership Page Design section, you’ll find ReadyLaunch’s customizable options.

Customizable Elements:

  • Background Colors and Images: Choose a custom background color or image that reflects your brand.
  • Font Styles: Change font types, sizes, and colors to match your site’s theme.
  • Button Colors: Customize the sign-up button colors and hover effects to stand out and improve conversion rates.

Preview Changes:

  • Use the built-in preview feature to see how your sign-up page will look across different devices (desktop, tablet, and mobile).
  • Adjust the design to ensure a smooth, user-friendly experience on all screen sizes.

2.2 Customize Pricing Pages

Your pricing page is where visitors can see your membership plans. ReadyLaunch helps you create a sleek, well-organized pricing page without any complex design work.

  1. Go to MemberPress → Settings → ReadyLaunch™ → Pricing Page.
  2. Enable the ReadyLaunch Pricing Page option.

Customizable Elements:

  • Membership Plan Layout: Choose from different layout styles (e.g., column-based, stacked).
  • Plan Features: Easily list out and customize the features of each membership level.
  • Highlight Best Plan: ReadyLaunch allows you to emphasize the most popular membership option by highlighting it.
  • Custom Call-to-Action (CTA): Modify the text and style of your CTA buttons (e.g., “Join Now!” or “Start Learning”).

Preview Changes:

  • Use the preview feature to see how your pricing table looks. Make sure it’s simple and clear for your users to understand the differences between membership levels.

2.3 Customize Login Pages

  1. Go to MemberPress → Settings → ReadyLaunch™ → Login Page.
  2. Enable the ReadyLaunch Login Page option.

Customizable Elements:

  • Background: Choose a color, gradient, or image for the login page background.
  • Logo Placement: Upload your company or website logo to appear at the top of the login form.
  • Form Style: Adjust the form fields’ style, including the text color, field borders, and button design.

This simple customization ensures a consistent branding experience from the moment users try to log in.

2.4 Customize Account Pages

  1. Go to MemberPress → Settings → ReadyLaunch™ → Account Page.
  2. Enable the ReadyLaunch Account Page option.

Customizable Elements:

  • User-Friendly Interface: The ReadyLaunch account page automatically organizes user information, subscription management, and billing details in an easy-to-navigate format.
  • Color Scheme: Adjust the color scheme to match your brand identity.
  • Font Adjustments: Change the font and text styling for easy readability.

3. 💻 Previewing and Finalizing Your Changes

Before going live, it’s crucial to preview how your changes look on your website.

  1. After customizing any page, you can use the Preview button to check the design.
  2. Make sure to preview on different devices (desktop, tablet, and mobile) to ensure a responsive and visually appealing layout on all screen sizes.
  3. Once satisfied with your design, click Save Changes to apply the updates to your live site.

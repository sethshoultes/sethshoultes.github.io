Customizing Your MemberPress Website Using ReadyLaunch

Tips for Customizing with ReadyLaunch

{% raw %}
  • Keep it simple: Minimalist designs with clear calls to action generally convert better. Avoid cluttering the page with too many elements.
  • Consistent branding: Make sure your color scheme, fonts, and logo are consistent across all pages for a professional and trustworthy look.
  • Responsive Design: Always test your changes across devices to ensure a smooth experience for both desktop and mobile users.
  • Emphasize Key Information: On your pricing and sign-up pages, ensure the most important information (like pricing details and the benefits of each membership tier) stands out.

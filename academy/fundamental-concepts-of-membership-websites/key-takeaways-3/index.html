Academy / Building Membership Websites with WordPress

Building a Hiking Membership Website Using MemberPress

Key Takeaways

{% raw %}
  1. MemberPress makes it easy to manage memberships, restrict content, and collect payments for your hiking membership website.
  2. Offer valuable exclusive content like trail guides, event registrations, and gear reviews to attract and retain members.
  3. Organize and promote hiking events to foster community and engagement among your members.
  4. Use email marketing to send regular updates, drip content, and event reminders.
  5. Monetize your site with digital products, affiliate marketing, and branded merchandise.
  6. Build a community forum where hikers can share experiences, tips, and recommendations.

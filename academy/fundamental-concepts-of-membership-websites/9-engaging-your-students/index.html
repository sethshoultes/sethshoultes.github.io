Academy / Building Membership Websites with WordPress

Guide to Building an Online Learning Platform Using MemberPress

Engaging Your Students

{% raw %}

Community Features

  • Consider using BuddyPress or PeepSo to create a social community around your learning platform.
  • Encourage students to engage in discussions, form study groups, or participate in Q&A forums.

Live Sessions and Webinars

  • Use plugins like WP Event Manager or Zoom Integration to host live classes, webinars, or Q&A sessions.
  • Promote these live events through automated MemberPress emails.

Gamification with GamiPress

  • Integrate GamiPress to award students points, badges, and achievements based on their course progress or quiz scores.
  • Gamification helps keep students motivated and engaged.

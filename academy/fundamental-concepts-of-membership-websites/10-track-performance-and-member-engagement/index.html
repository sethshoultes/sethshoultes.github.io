Academy / Building Membership Websites with WordPress

Building a Hiking Membership Website Using MemberPress

10. Track Performance and Member Engagement

{% raw %}

Use MemberPress and other plugins to monitor the performance of your hiking membership site.

10.1 MemberPress Reports

  • Track Revenue: View how much money you're making from memberships, digital product sales, and event registrations.
  • Track Member Activity: See which content members are engaging with the most (e.g., popular trail guides, events).

10.2 Google Analytics

  • Install Google Analytics to track traffic and user behavior on your website.
  • Monitor which content is driving the most sign-ups and where your visitors are coming from.

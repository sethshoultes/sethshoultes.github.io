Accessibility and SEO Best Practices

Why Accessibility and SEO Matter

{% raw %}

Accessibility:

Accessibility ensures that your website is usable by people with various disabilities, such as visual, auditory, motor, or cognitive impairments. According to the World Health Organization (WHO), over 1 billion people globally live with some form of disability, making accessibility crucial for providing equal access to your website’s content.

SEO:

SEO (Search Engine Optimization) improves the visibility of your website on search engines like Google. SEO-friendly practices help search engines understand the content on your site, making it more likely to rank well for relevant queries. Additionally, SEO best practices often align with accessibility, as both aim to improve the usability and user experience of websites.

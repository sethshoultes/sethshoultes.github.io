Web Performance Optimization

1. Why Web Performance Matters

{% raw %}

Web performance has a direct impact on the user experience. Slow-loading websites can lead to higher bounce rates, lower engagement, and even a drop in search engine rankings. Studies show that:

  • 40% of users abandon a website if it takes more than 3 seconds to load.
  • Google uses page speed as a ranking factor, meaning a slow website can negatively affect your SEO.
  • Faster websites lead to higher conversion rates and better user retention.

Accessibility and SEO Best Practices

{% raw %}

In modern web development, two critical factors that significantly affect the success of your website are accessibility and SEO (Search Engine Optimization). Both work towards improving the user experience and making websites more discoverable, usable, and inclusive for all visitors. Accessibility ensures that your site is usable by people with disabilities, while SEO helps search engines understand and rank your content, improving visibility in search results.

In this section, we’ll explore the best practices for creating accessible, SEO-friendly websites, ensuring that your content is both discoverable by search engines and usable for all users.

Rubik's Cube Maneuver: The Ultimate Freestyle Drone Trick

6. Common Mistakes to Avoid

{% raw %}

6.1 Losing Altitude

  • One of the most common mistakes in the Rubik’s Cube is losing altitude during the rapid sequence of flips and rolls. This happens when throttle is reduced too much or not increased at the right time.

How to Fix: Practice maintaining steady throttle control throughout the maneuver. Focus on keeping the throttle slightly elevated during the flips to prevent the drone from dropping too low.

6.2 Overshooting the Rolls or Flips

  • Another common issue is overshooting the rolls or flips, where the drone rotates too far or too fast, making it difficult to complete the sequence cleanly.

How to Fix: Practice controlling the stick inputs to ensure that each movement is deliberate and precise. Focus on stopping the roll or flip at the right point to maintain control.

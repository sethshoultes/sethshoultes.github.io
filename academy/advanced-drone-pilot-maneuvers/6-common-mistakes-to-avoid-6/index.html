Acrobatic Spiral: Mastering the Complex Spiral Dive

6. Common Mistakes to Avoid

{% raw %}

6.1 Losing Control of the Spiral

  • A common mistake in the Acrobatic Spiral is losing control, where the drone starts to drift off its spiral path or wobbles during the rotation.

How to Fix: Focus on keeping your yaw and roll inputs steady. Avoid over-correcting with large stick movements. Small, smooth inputs will help maintain control over the spiral.

6.2 Descending Too Quickly

  • Another issue is descending too quickly during the spiral, which can result in a hard landing or crash if throttle control is not managed properly.

How to Fix: Gradually reduce throttle during descending spirals, and be ready to increase throttle near the end of the maneuver to recover smoothly.

Inverted Yaw Spin: Mastering the Ultimate Freestyle FPV Trick

3. Advanced Inverted Yaw Spin Techniques

{% raw %}

Once you’re comfortable with the basic Inverted Yaw Spin, you can incorporate advanced variations to make the maneuver more dynamic and impressive.

3.1 Slow Inverted Yaw Spin

A slow Inverted Yaw Spin emphasizes the visual impact of the spin by reducing the speed of both the yaw rotation and throttle inputs, creating a slow-motion effect. This variation is great for cinematic FPV flying, where you want to highlight smooth, controlled movements.

How to Perform a Slow Inverted Yaw Spin:

  • Apply Gentle Yaw Input: Instead of spinning quickly, use slower, more gradual yaw inputs to create a slower, more deliberate rotation.
  • Throttle Stability: Keep throttle input steady to maintain altitude during the slow spin.

Example:

A slow Inverted Yaw Spin over a landscape or a large object like a building or a mountain creates a stunning, panoramic view from an inverted perspective.

3.2 Tight Inverted Yaw Spin

A tight Inverted Yaw Spin involves spinning quickly in a compact, controlled circle. This variation requires faster yaw input and precise throttle management to maintain control while spinning rapidly.

How to Perform a Tight Inverted Yaw Spin:

  • Fast Yaw Movements: Use sharp, quick yaw inputs to create a tight, rapid spin while the drone remains inverted.
  • Throttle Control: Be mindful of throttle input—rapid spins can make it harder to maintain altitude, so adjust throttle accordingly.

Example:

Tight Inverted Yaw Spins are commonly used in freestyle flying when performing stunts around small objects like poles or trees, adding excitement to the routine with quick, controlled spins.

3.3 Inverted Yaw Spin with Descent

In this variation, you combine the yaw spin with a slow, controlled descent, allowing the drone to spiral downward while inverted. This adds complexity and requires excellent throttle management to keep the descent smooth.

How to Perform an Inverted Yaw Spin with Descent:

  • Yaw Spin Initiation: Begin with the standard Inverted Yaw Spin, applying steady yaw input while inverted.
  • Throttle Reduction: Gradually reduce throttle to start descending while maintaining the spin. Be careful not to cut throttle too much or the drone could descend too rapidly.
  • Control the Descent: As the drone spins and descends, keep a close eye on altitude. Make small throttle adjustments to ensure the descent remains slow and controlled.

Example:

An Inverted Yaw Spin with descent is perfect for cinematic FPV flying, where you want to combine vertical movement with rotation, such as spiraling down the side of a building or mountain.

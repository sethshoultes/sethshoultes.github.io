Acrobatic Spiral: Mastering the Complex Spiral Dive

Steps to Perform an Acrobatic Spiral

{% raw %}

Here’s a step-by-step guide to executing a smooth and controlled Acrobatic Spiral:

Step 1: Set Your Flight Path

  • Choose Your Path: Identify the object or area you want to spiral around, such as a pole, tree, or open space. Ensure the object is centered in your intended flight path.
  • Position the Drone: Start by positioning the drone at an appropriate distance from the object to give yourself enough space for the spiral. You’ll also want enough altitude if performing a descending spiral.

Step 2: Initiate the Spiral

  • Begin with Yaw and Roll: To start the spiral, simultaneously apply yaw (left stick) and roll (right stick) inputs in the same direction (either left or right). The yaw will cause the drone to rotate around its vertical axis, while the roll will maintain the circular flight path.
  • Throttle Management: Keep the throttle steady to maintain altitude if you’re performing a level or ascending spiral. If you’re descending, gradually reduce throttle to control the descent speed.

Step 3: Tighten or Loosen the Spiral

  • Tighten the Spiral: To make the spiral tighter, increase the yaw and roll inputs, making the drone rotate and turn faster. Be mindful of throttle to ensure you don’t lose too much altitude or control during the faster rotation.
  • Loosen the Spiral: To create a wider, more gradual spiral, reduce the yaw and roll inputs, allowing the drone to circle more slowly and in a wider arc.

Step 4: Maintain Smooth Control

  • Focus on Smooth Inputs: Keep your stick movements steady and consistent to maintain a smooth spiral path. Sudden or jerky inputs can cause the drone to wobble or drift off course.
  • Adjust Throttle as Needed: If the spiral is descending, gradually reduce throttle while maintaining enough power to keep the descent smooth. For ascending spirals, increase throttle slightly to counteract any loss of altitude.

Step 5: Add Complex Elements (Optional)

  • Introduce Flips or Rolls: To make the maneuver more complex, you can introduce flips or rolls during the spiral. For example, you can perform a roll or flip midway through the spiral to add dynamic motion while keeping the spiral path.
  • Smooth Recovery: Once the spiral is complete, center the sticks and adjust throttle to recover smoothly and continue flying forward.

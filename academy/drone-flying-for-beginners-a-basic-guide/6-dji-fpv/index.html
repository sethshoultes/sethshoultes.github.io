Academy / Drone Flying for Beginners: A Basic Guide

Overview of the DJI Drone Line: Mini, Avata, Mavic 3, and More

DJI FPV

{% raw %}

Price: ~$1,299 USD
Weight: 795g
Camera: 4K/60fps video
Flight Time: Up to 20 minutes

The DJI FPV drone is designed to offer a blend of immersive FPV flying and high-quality video recording. While it’s a bit larger and heavier than the DJI Avata, it provides a more traditional FPV experience, with full manual control and incredible speed.

Key Features:

  • 4K/60fps Video: Captures high-quality footage at 120Mbps, ensuring clear and detailed recordings during fast flights.
  • High Speed: Capable of reaching speeds up to 87 mph in Manual Mode, making it perfect for FPV racing and freestyle flying.
  • Emergency Brake and Hover: Even in Manual Mode, you can instantly stop and hover with the press of a button, adding safety to high-speed FPV flying.

Who It’s For:

  • Designed for FPV pilots who want to experience high-speed flying with professional video quality.
  • Great for action sports videographers and FPV racers.

Example Use:

Perfect for fast-paced FPV racing, flying through mountain ranges, or creating adrenaline-packed action shots from a first-person perspective.

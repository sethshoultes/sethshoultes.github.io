Drone Flying Tests and Simulations

Hands-On Simulations in Real Life

{% raw %}

1. Mini Drone Practice

If you already own a drone or a mini drone, set up a simple obstacle course in your home or backyard. Here’s what you can do:

  • Fly around household objects (like chairs or tables) without touching them.
  • Practice taking off and landing in small, designated areas.
  • Perform slow and controlled turns and hovering exercises.

2. Outdoor Flying Practice

Once you feel confident in a simulator, head out to an open space and try these real-life challenges:

  • Hovering Test: Try to keep your drone at a consistent height without moving in any direction.
  • Box Test: Fly your drone in a square or box pattern, focusing on precise corner turns.
  • Figure-8: Fly in a figure-8 pattern to improve your control over the yaw and pitch.

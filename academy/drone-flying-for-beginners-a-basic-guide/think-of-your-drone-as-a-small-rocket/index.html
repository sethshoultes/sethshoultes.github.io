Drone Flying Examples and Metaphors

Think of Your Drone as a Small Rocket

{% raw %}

Imagine your drone as a small rocket that you can control:

  • Throttle is like your rocket's fuel: the more throttle, the higher it flies. However, unlike a rocket, you can control when to stop going higher, keep it steady, or bring it back down safely.
  • Directional Controls (pitch, roll, yaw) give your drone more freedom than a simple rocket. You’re not just shooting up; you can move left, right, forward, or backward—like steering a spaceship!

Why These Rules Matter

{% raw %}

Safety First

Flying a drone might seem simple, but safety is crucial. You’re operating a machine that can weigh several pounds, and if something goes wrong, it could crash into people or property.

Legal Responsibility

Just like driving a car, drone pilots must follow the law. Failure to follow regulations can lead to heavy fines or even confiscation of your drone.

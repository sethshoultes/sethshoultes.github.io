🏫 Drone Flight Schools

Many aspiring professional drone pilots enroll in specialized drone flight schools, which offer hands-on training and instruction in various professional applications. These schools provide:

  • Simulated flight missions that mimic real-world tasks, such as mapping a construction site or filming a commercial.
  • Flight theory lessons: Pilots learn how to navigate different terrains, operate in adverse weather, and manage long-duration flights.
  • Hands-on flight experience: Instructors teach pilots how to maneuver drones in complex environments, focusing on precision and safety.

🎓 Online Drone Pilot Training Programs

Some training is offered online, with virtual classes and flight simulators. This is especially common for certifications like FAA Part 107 or EASA A2. Popular online drone schools include:

  • Drone Pilot Ground School: A comprehensive Part 107 course offering both theoretical lessons and practice tests.
  • UAV Coach: Offers Part 107 preparation as well as courses in aerial photography and real estate applications.

6. Drone Racing

{% raw %}

Drones have also evolved into a sport called drone racing. Pilots wear first-person view (FPV) goggles, allowing them to see what the drone sees as it zips through obstacle courses at high speeds.

🏁 Drone Racing Leagues

  • In drone racing leagues, pilots compete to navigate drones through obstacle courses as fast as possible, often at speeds of up to 100 mph (160 km/h)!
  • This is a highly skilled and competitive sport, requiring fast reflexes and expert control.

If you enjoy high-speed thrills, FPV racing could be a fun and challenging way to advance your drone flying skills.

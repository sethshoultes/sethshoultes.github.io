DJI Avata: First-Person View (FPV) Experience

{% raw %}

Price: ~$629 USD (Drone only, no goggles or controller)
Weight: 410g
Camera: 4K/60fps
Flight Time: Up to 18 minutes

The DJI Avata is designed for FPV (First-Person View) enthusiasts who want a more immersive flying experience. Paired with DJI FPV Goggles 2 or DJI Goggles Integra, the Avata lets you feel like you’re inside the drone as you soar through tight spaces, forests, or indoor environments.

Key Features:

  • 4K/60fps Video: Records high-quality 4K video with RockSteady 2.0 and HorizonSteady stabilization for smooth, cinematic footage.
  • Cinewhoop Design: Avata has built-in ducted propellers, making it durable and capable of flying in tight spaces, even indoors.
  • Manual Mode (Acro Mode): Advanced pilots can switch to Manual Mode for full control, allowing them to perform flips, rolls, and other acrobatic maneuvers.
  • Turtle Mode: If the drone flips over, Turtle Mode allows it to right itself and continue flying, which is especially useful for FPV racing and freestyle flying.

Who It’s For:

  • Perfect for FPV enthusiasts and those looking to enter the world of immersive first-person drone flying.
  • Great for action videographers and freestyle pilots who want the thrill of flying through obstacles with complete freedom.

Example Use:

Best for dynamic shots in tight spaces, such as flying through buildings, forests, or racing tracks, where the first-person perspective adds excitement and immersion.

3. DJI Mavic 3 Series

{% raw %}

DJI Mavic 3

Price: ~$2,049 USD (Standard Version)
Weight: 895g
Camera: 5.1K video, 20MP Hasselblad camera
Flight Time: Up to 46 minutes

The DJI Mavic 3 is a top-tier drone for professionals, offering incredible image quality, long flight time, and advanced features like omnidirectional obstacle sensing. It’s the go-to drone for filmmakers, photographers, and content creators who demand the best in aerial imaging.

Key Features:

  • Dual Camera System: Features a Hasselblad 4/3 CMOS camera for stunning image quality and a 12MP telephoto lens for zoom capabilities.
  • 5.1K Video Recording: Shoots 5.1K/50fps video and 4K/120fps slow-motion footage, making it ideal for cinematic projects.
  • Advanced Obstacle Sensing: Equipped with omnidirectional obstacle sensors, providing 360-degree protection to avoid collisions during complex maneuvers.
  • 46-Minute Flight Time: One of the longest flight times available, allowing extended shooting sessions without frequent battery changes.

Who It’s For:

  • Designed for professional filmmakers, aerial photographers, and content creators who need the highest image quality and advanced flight features.
  • Ideal for real estate videography, film production, and surveying.

Example Use:

The Mavic 3 is perfect for high-end film projects, such as shooting cinematic landscapes, real estate marketing videos, or surveying large properties for construction planning.

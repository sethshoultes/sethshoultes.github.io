Examples and Metaphors for Understanding HTML/CSS

Summary

{% raw %}

HTML is the structure, whether it’s the skeleton of a house, the ingredients of a burger, or the frame of a car.

CSS is the style, how the house is painted, how the burger looks on the plate, or what color and materials are used in the car.

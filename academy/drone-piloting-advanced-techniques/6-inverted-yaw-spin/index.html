Inverted Yaw Spin

{% raw %}

An Inverted Yaw Spin is exactly what it sounds like: a controlled spin while the drone is upside down. This move is challenging because you’ll need to manage both yaw and pitch in reverse while keeping the drone stable.

How to Perform an Inverted Yaw Spin:

  1. Invert the Drone: Start by performing a half-flip (pitch the drone forward or backward) so that it’s upside down.
  2. Apply Yaw: Once inverted, apply yaw by moving the left stick to the left or right to initiate the spin.
  3. Maintain Control: As the drone spins, manage throttle and slight pitch inputs to prevent the drone from losing too much altitude or drifting.

Example:

This trick is commonly used in freestyle flying when pilots want to add flair to their routines. It’s often combined with other acrobatic moves, like a Matty Flip or Power Loop, for more complexity.

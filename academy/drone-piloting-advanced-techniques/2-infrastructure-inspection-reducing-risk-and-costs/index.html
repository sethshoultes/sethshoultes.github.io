2. Infrastructure Inspection: Reducing Risk and Costs

{% raw %}

Company: Cyberhawk Innovations Project: Drone-Based Industrial Inspections

Case Study:

Cyberhawk is a world leader in drone-based inspections, particularly for critical infrastructure such as offshore oil rigs, wind turbines, and powerlines. Traditionally, inspecting these structures involved sending personnel to climb or rappel, which was time-consuming and dangerous.

Innovative Use:

  • Remote Inspection: Drones equipped with high-resolution cameras and thermal sensors can inspect hard-to-reach areas without putting human workers at risk. They can quickly assess damage or wear, ensuring timely maintenance and reducing the chance of failure.
  • 3D Modeling: After flying a drone around a structure, the images are processed into a 3D model of the site, allowing engineers to assess the condition remotely and plan repairs.

Example:

Cyberhawk used drones to inspect a large oil refinery in the Middle East, completing the job in 50% less time than traditional methods, with no downtime for the facility. The drone inspection revealed several critical issues that were repaired before they could escalate, saving millions in potential damages.

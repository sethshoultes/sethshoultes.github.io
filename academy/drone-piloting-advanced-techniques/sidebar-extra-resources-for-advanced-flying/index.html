Academy / Drone Piloting: Advanced Techniques

Advanced Maneuvers and Techniques for Expert Drone Pilots

Sidebar: Extra Resources for Advanced Flying

{% raw %}
  • 🔼 Advanced FPV Maneuvers: Watch videos from top FPV freestyle pilots to see these tricks in action and learn how they perfect each move.
  • 💡 Drone Simulators: Practice with Liftoff or Velocidrone to develop muscle memory for advanced acro tricks without the risk of damaging your drone.
  • 📚 FPV Drone Tuning: Learn how to tweak your drone’s flight controller settings in BetaFlight or EmuFlight for better control during advanced maneuvers.
  • 🧪 Flight Practice Drills: Set up structured practice drills, such as obstacle courses or figure-eights, to sharpen your reflexes and control.
  • ➕ FPV Drone Racing: Explore drone racing leagues and competitions to apply your advanced skills in high-speed, competitive environments.
{% endraw %}

Drone Regulations: Understanding Legal Challenges and Certifications

1. General Overview of Global Drone Regulations

{% raw %}

Drone regulations vary widely from country to country, but the main objectives are to:

  • Ensure public safety: Prevent collisions with aircraft, protect people on the ground, and avoid damage to property.
  • Preserve privacy: Protect citizens from unauthorized surveillance or data collection.
  • Manage airspace: Coordinate drone flights with manned aircraft to prevent interference, especially near airports.

Most countries classify drones based on their weight and purpose (recreational vs. commercial), with stricter rules applying to drones used for commercial purposes.

Academy / Drone Piloting: Advanced Techniques

Certification Study Guides: FAA Part 107 and EU A2 Certificate

Practice Tests and Online Resources

{% raw %}

Here are some valuable practice test resources and study aids for both FAA and EU drone certifications:

FAA Part 107 Practice Tests:

  • FAA Sample Exam: The FAA offers a free sample exam that mirrors the Part 107 knowledge test. You can take it here.
  • RemotePilot101: This site offers full Part 107 training courses, study guides, and practice exams. It’s a great resource for structured learning.
  • 3DR Practice Tests: 3DR, a drone company, offers interactive Part 107 practice tests online. These are helpful for testing your knowledge in real-time conditions.

EU A2 Certificate Practice Tests:

  • Drone Academy EU: Provides comprehensive A2 exam preparation courses and practice tests, covering all topics from airspace regulations to risk assessments.
  • UAVHub: Offers courses and study materials specific to the A2 Certificate, including video tutorials, mock exams, and real-world scenarios to test your knowledge.
  • EASA Guides: The EASA provides official materials and resources. Check out the European Union Drone Portal for practice tests and official updates on drone regulations across the EU.
{% endraw %}

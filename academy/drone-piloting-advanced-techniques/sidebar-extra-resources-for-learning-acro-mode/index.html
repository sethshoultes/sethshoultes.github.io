Academy / Drone Piloting: Advanced Techniques

Advanced Techniques: Mastering Acro Mode for Full Manual Control

Sidebar: Extra Resources for Learning Acro Mode

{% raw %}
  • 🔼 Watch Pro Pilots in Acro Mode: Study videos of pro FPV pilots performing acrobatic stunts in Acro Mode to see how they handle yaw, pitch, and throttle.
  • 💡 Flight Simulators: Use simulators like Liftoff or DRL Simulator to practice Acro Mode without risking damage to your drone.
  • 📚 Learn Flight Dynamics: Deepen your understanding of drone flight dynamics, such as thrust-to-weight ratio, to enhance your Acro Mode performance.
  • 🧪 Acro Mode Training Drills: Practice training drills that focus on stick control, like figure-eights and slow rolls, to build confidence in manual flight.
  • ➕ Join FPV Communities: Connect with FPV communities online for tips, advice, and challenges related to Acro Mode flying.

{% endraw %}

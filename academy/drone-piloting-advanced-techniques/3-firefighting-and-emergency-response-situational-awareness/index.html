3. Firefighting and Emergency Response: Situational Awareness

{% raw %}

Company: DJI Enterprise (Drone Manufacturer) Project: Firefighting in Australia

Case Study:

During the devastating 2019-2020 Australian bushfires, DJI Enterprise drones played a critical role in helping firefighting teams and emergency responders.

Innovative Use:

  • Thermal Imaging: Drones equipped with thermal cameras were used to track the spread of fires in real-time, even through heavy smoke, helping teams determine the hottest areas and where the fire was heading.
  • Search and Rescue: UAVs also assisted in locating trapped individuals by using infrared sensors to detect body heat, allowing rescuers to navigate dangerous areas safely and quickly.
  • Post-Fire Assessment: After fires were contained, drones were deployed to assess the damage and provide a detailed map of the affected areas for disaster recovery planning.

Example:

In New South Wales, Australia, DJI drones were used to create thermal maps of the fire’s perimeter. Firefighters used this data to prioritize their efforts and prevent the fire from reaching populated areas. The drones also saved over 200 hours of helicopter use, reducing operational costs significantly.

Advanced Techniques: Mastering Acro Mode for Full Manual Control

Advanced Acro Mode Maneuvers

{% raw %}

Once you’ve mastered basic rolls and flips, you can move on to complex acrobatic moves used by professional FPV pilots. These maneuvers require precise timing and stick control but will push your skills to the next level.

Power Loops

  • How to Execute:
    1. Start by flying forward with moderate throttle.
    2. Pitch back and increase throttle to loop over a virtual object (like a tree or pole).
    3. As the drone climbs and flips backward, reduce throttle slightly to complete the loop.
  • Key Tip: Practice doing power loops over an open field before introducing real-world obstacles. Controlling the throttle throughout the maneuver ensures that you stay on track.

Split-S Maneuver

  • How to Execute:
    1. Start by flying forward at moderate speed.
    2. Yaw to face the opposite direction (180-degree turn).
    3. Perform a half-roll while pitching back to rapidly reverse direction and altitude.
  • Key Tip: The Split-S is used to quickly change direction while dropping altitude, making it useful in drone racing or when navigating tight spaces.

Inverted Hover

  • How to Execute:
    1. Roll the drone upside down and try to hover in this inverted state.
    2. Use reverse pitch and throttle inputs to keep the drone stable.
  • Key Tip: Maintaining an inverted hover requires high precision. Any slight mistake in throttle control can cause the drone to rapidly descend or lose control.

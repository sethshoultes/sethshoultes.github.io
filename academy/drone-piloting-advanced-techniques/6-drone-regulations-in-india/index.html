Drone Regulations in India

{% raw %}

Regulatory Body: Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA)

Drone Classes and Requirements:

India divides drones into five weight-based categories, ranging from Nano (<250g) to Large (>25kg).

Key Requirements:

  1. Registration and Licensing: All drones except Nano drones require Unmanned Aircraft Operator Permits (UAOP) for commercial use.
  2. Remote Pilot License: Pilots must have a Remote Pilot License issued by the DGCA for all categories except Nano and Micro drones used for recreational purposes.
  3. No-Fly Zones: Restricted areas include airports, military installations, and national parks.
  4. Altitude Limit: Drones must stay below 400 feet and maintain visual line of sight.

Example:

An agricultural drone spraying crops over large fields would need to meet Medium drone registration requirements, as well as obtain a Remote Pilot License and adhere to no-fly zone restrictions.

Company: SkySpecs Project: Autonomous Wind Turbine Inspections

Case Study:

SkySpecs is a drone company that specializes in automated wind turbine inspections. Wind turbines, especially offshore ones, are difficult to access and inspect regularly, making drones the perfect solution.

Innovative Use:

  • Automated Inspections: SkySpecs drones are capable of fully autonomous inspections of wind turbines. Once launched, the drones fly a predefined route around the turbine, capturing high-resolution images of the blades and structure.
  • AI-Powered Analysis: The images captured by the drones are then processed using AI algorithms to detect cracks, corrosion, and other defects, providing detailed reports to maintenance teams.

Example:

SkySpecs deployed drones to inspect over 5,000 wind turbines across the United States and Europe. Their solution reduced inspection times by 80%, increased safety by eliminating the need for human inspectors to climb turbines, and provided precise data for predictive maintenance.

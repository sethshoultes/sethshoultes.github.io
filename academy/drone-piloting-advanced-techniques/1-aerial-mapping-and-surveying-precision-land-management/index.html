Company: SenseFly (Drone Manufacturer) Project: Agricultural Surveying with eBee Drones

Case Study:

SenseFly is a leader in agricultural surveying, providing drone solutions for farmers to manage crops more efficiently. Using their fixed-wing eBee X drones, SenseFly enables farmers to perform large-scale surveys over vast areas in a fraction of the time it would take with traditional ground-based methods.

Innovative Use:

  • Precision Mapping: The drone captures high-resolution aerial images and multi-spectral data, which is processed to create detailed maps showing variations in soil health, crop density, and water usage.
  • Data-Driven Decisions: Farmers use these maps to apply fertilizers more precisely, monitor crop growth, and manage irrigation systems, reducing waste and improving yield.

Example:

A sugarcane farm in Brazil used SenseFly's drones to scan a 2,000-hectare field in just a few hours. The drones provided detailed maps showing crop health, which allowed the farmers to target problem areas, resulting in a 10% increase in yield.

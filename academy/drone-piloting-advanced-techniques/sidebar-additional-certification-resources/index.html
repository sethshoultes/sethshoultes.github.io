Academy / Drone Piloting: Advanced Techniques

Certification Study Guides: FAA Part 107 and EU A2 Certificate

Sidebar: Additional Certification Resources

{% raw %}
  • 🔼 Study Apps: Download study apps like UAV Ground School (for Part 107) and Drone License Prep (for EU A2) to study on the go.
  • 💡 Interactive Flight Simulators: Use simulators to practice flight maneuvers and learn to respond to emergency situations in controlled environments.
  • 📚 Video Tutorials: Watch YouTube channels like Pilot Institute for step-by-step guides on passing the Part 107 exam, or Drone Masterclass for EU-specific tutorials.
  • 🧪 Practice with Real Drones: Get practical flight time using drones equipped with cameras and sensors to apply what you’ve learned in both the FAA and EU exams.
  • ➕ FAA Part 107 Waivers: Learn more about how to apply for Part 107 waivers, such as those for BVLOS (Beyond Visual Line of Sight) flights, and what conditions must be met.
{% endraw %}

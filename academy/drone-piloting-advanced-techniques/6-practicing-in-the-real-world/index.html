Academy / Drone Piloting: Advanced Techniques

Advanced Techniques: Mastering Acro Mode for Full Manual Control

Practicing in the Real World

{% raw %}

Once you’re comfortable with Acro Mode in a simulator, it’s time to practice in the real world. Here’s how to safely transition:

  • Start High: Always begin practicing new maneuvers at higher altitudes to give yourself time to recover from mistakes.
  • Choose Open Areas: Fly in wide, open fields where there are no obstacles like trees or buildings.
  • Slow and Steady: Don’t rush into advanced flips or rolls. Start with basic hovering, then progress to slow rolls and flips.

Safety Tip:

Before flying in Acro Mode, ensure that your drone’s Fail-Safe or Return-to-Home functions are enabled in case of signal loss or emergencies.

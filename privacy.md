# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 11, 2026*

This policy covers "Seth's clone", a private assistant that Seth Shoultes runs for himself. It is not offered to the public. The only Google accounts it connects to are Seth's own.

## What it reads

With Seth's permission, the clone reads email (Gmail) and calendar events (Google Calendar) in his own accounts. It uses them to:

- sort and label his email,
- move old newsletters to Trash (Gmail keeps them there for 30 days, so they can be recovered),
- write him a daily summary of what needs his attention.

It never deletes anything permanently, and it never touches starred messages.

## Where your data lives

What the clone keeps is stored in Seth's own Google Cloud project. The sign-in tokens are kept in Google Secret Manager. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone else, and nothing is used for advertising.

## Google user data

The clone's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

Seth can remove the clone's access at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Removing it stops all reading straight away.
